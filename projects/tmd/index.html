Mutation by Natural Dominance

Which evolutionary route occurs depends on more than the supply of mutations. TMD explores how context, accessibility, establishment and observation affect the routes we see, with public calculations and tests that can be inspected.

Research status

Exploratory research. A source review and control plan now distinguish accurate variant calls from evidence of a new mutation origin. Exact synthetic methods test detection rules requiring multiple supporting descendants and reference calls. Equal cell recovery can still leave a true event unobserved when the required calls do not coincide. These advances have been independently checked internally; qualified biological and statistical review remains pending. Actual cancer categories, axis, matched system, laboratory partner, native controls and protocol remain unchosen. All 26 actual bacterial study inputs remain unresolved. Biological validation and cancer prevention have not been established. The labelled methods archive remains 0.5.0; the prepared 0.6.0 package is unpublished.

What the public work reports

Variant accuracy differs from origin accuracy

A correct inherited variant can still be wrongly assigned as a new mutation origin. The control plan therefore asks for distinct evidence on genotype errors, ancestry and acquisition episodes, missing lineages and transfer into the actual biological system. Published coverage, conditional classifier sensitivity and agreement between assays support different claims. The packet is ready for qualified assessment; no external review or experiment has been obtained.

Support and reference rules can hide true events

In a synthetic model, two descendants each have a one-half chance of a correct call. A rule requiring both can have inclusion probability one-half, one-quarter or zero, depending on their joint calls. Requiring a reference call adds another dependence condition. Sharp small-model bounds have exact attaining examples and independent certificates. These rules illustrate a measurement problem; they do not reconstruct a native caller, infer unseen births or validate cancer prevention.

A reviewable cell-lineage study contract

The new pedigree ledger distinguishes observed divisions, inferred origin intervals, inherited copies and unknown genotypes. A clearly labelled synthetic example keeps dead and lost lineages visible and separates branch-supported hypotheses from ambiguous leaf observations. The expert-review packet asks qualified biologists and statisticians to assess actual event definitions, native caller rules, controls, replication and inference before a pilot. No laboratory protocol or experiment has been commissioned.

Equal cell recovery can give unequal event detection

In a synthetic validated-call model, two descendants each have a one-half chance of providing a true call. Their shared mutation can have detection probability one-half, three-quarters or one, depending on joint recovery. Repeated inherited copies remain one birth. Exact bounds and classical fixed-event sampling calculations make these assumptions explicit; they do not reveal unknown births or fit a biological mutation rate. The any-call rule is separate from a caller requiring multiple subclone support.

Observed pedigrees offer a concrete measurement lead

A published epithelial cell-line experiment combines optical division tracking with sequencing of related descendants. Eleven of 45 available HT115 channel cells and 13 of 26 RPE1 cells produced primary sequence samples. The 24 subclones descend from two displayed founders; these realized nested fractions are not calibrated route capture or independent mutation births. Lost genotypes and unmatched cell backgrounds keep this study outside a confirmatory TMD test.

Bounded uncertainty separates unresolved patterns from incompatible ones

Under an explicitly admitted expected-mass model and positive independent Cartesian parameter bounds, exact curvature intervals show whether some common value remains possible. Wide overlap is inconclusive; empty intersection excludes the restricted model only under those population assumptions. A separate proportionality check asks whether ordinary supply, growth and recovery explain all three routes. Passing the necessary curvature condition alone is weaker. No biological confidence interval, count law, power or prevention effect is supplied.

A cancer-relevant test needs independent lineage evidence

Two foundational studies make strong competing explanations concrete. A human normal-esophagus study maps 844 spatial samples from nine donors; detectable cancer-associated clones are distinct from malignant transformation or counted new mutation births. A mouse study tests competition between normal epithelial clones and early lesions, with observations, interventions and simulations kept separate. Its authors leave analogous human tumor elimination unknown. These established results guide a proposed measurement contract; they do not validate TMD.

Ordinary growth or recovery can produce the same endpoint pattern

Exact synthetic examples keep mutation supply fixed while changing relative terminal yield or detection. The supply-adjusted curvature remains common across contexts. This concerns normalized expected endpoint composition, not a complete lineage, count or first-arrival law. A useful test needs independently measured alternatives and predictions fixed before the held-out outcomes. Cancer routes and their ordered axis are still unchosen.

Reporting scale matters before biological inference

A declared clinical supplement remains unacquired after four distinct retrieval attempts. A conditional synthetic example has a raw midpoint median of 50 and a back-transformed log midpoint of 10 after zero handling. Another separates a ratio of marginal medians from a paired effect. These examples preserve the published numbers and do not authenticate the paper's actual reporting convention or calibrate biological recovery.

Cells, colonies and recovery need separate denominators

A bounded source screen examines two primary main-text methods/results studies: processing of 72 clinical urine specimens and persistence assays in P. aeruginosa PA14 and clinical isolates. Twenty measurement-ledger rows separate specimens, flow events, regrowth and CFUs. Membrane-integrity counts and colony formation are different outcomes; a nonsignificant comparison does not establish equivalence. Reported marginal CFU medians are not paired recovery probabilities. Neither source calibrates capture for SBW25 or the three TMD routes. NanoSpacer remains an abstract-only lead.

What known absolute capture could identify

Under a finite compound-Poisson model, known independent per-unit capture, a justified bound on strictly positive terminal marks and the complete population jump-intensity vector permit triangular inversion of the positive mark weights and intensity. This is ideal conditional identification, not an estimator from a finite culture histogram. Sharp void-probability bounds are deterministic model bounds, not observed confidence intervals. The producer passes 1,624 exact synthetic checks; independent internal review passes 10,747 invariant checks and 20,154 producer comparisons across 960 synthetic cases. These are computational checks, not biological observations. No biological capture, mutation rate or new theorem is established.

The remaining invisible events and unit ambiguity

Unknown capture can leave different event intensities with the same complete observed law. Even at known capture, extra precursor events with zero terminal descendants remain invisible. Identification of positive terminal intensity does not recover those lost events or prove that a CFU represents one cell. Ancestry, imported alleles, growth, recovery and native/reporter transfer still need matched measurements. No actual study field or biological calibration is supplied.

Authenticate estimator inputs before fitting

In the same released SBW25 source data, nine of twelve wild-type culture rows yield fractional genotype-corrected candidate-pool estimates. Candidate colonies, confirmed colonies, CFU densities and nominal whole-culture totals remain different quantities. Their reconstruction does not authenticate what was submitted to FALCOR or establish a software error. The historical input report, software build and estimator settings remain unverified; no biological mutation-rate fit is justified.

A paired check of the observation process

Under independently justified disjoint assignment from one culture, allocation between two samples conditional on their total follows a binomial split law. Shared-culture disjoint samples, overlapping recapture and independent cultures can share marginals while differing jointly. Exact finite examples pass 8,208 producer checks and 8,200 independently implemented review checks. Those numbers count computational assertions, not biological trials. No paired biological data, rate fit or confidence interval is supplied.

Passing a technical split check has limits

Many terminal histories can pass the conditional allocation check, including imported alleles, common unmeasured loss and sampling of multicell CFU packets. Passing does not uniquely authenticate molecular origin, absolute recovery, individual-cell units or the sampling frame. An explicit model allowing precursor events with zero terminal descendants changes latent event intensity while preserving the full descendant-count distribution. This is a conditional counterexample, not a general claim that every strictly positive clone model is unidentifiable.

Nominal plate geometry and conditional detection

The SBW25 source recipe and workbook reconstruct sampling fractions for all 24 culture occurrences: 0.0075%–0.075% for the reporter background and 7.5% for the deletion background. These are nominal aliquot fractions, not calibrated detection probabilities. A classical clone-and-sampling benchmark shows why zero observations alone can confound mutation intensity with clone survival and capture. Full count distributions can add information under a justified model. All new numerical examples are conditional and synthetic; no biological mutation-rate fit, new theorem or outward numerical certificate is claimed.

An inherited allele can enter without a new mutation

The E.coli–P1 paper behind the earlier abstract lead has now been read at full-main-text depth. Its reported roughly 380-fold excess of recovered recombinant over mutant CFUs at 24 hours concerns descendant abundance, not independently counted molecular event rates. Imported donor alleles, newly created mutations and standing variation have different origins and denominators. The source supports an assay-specific transfer mechanism; it does not establish transfer in SBW25. Supplementary models and raw data were not acquired or reproduced.

Public reporter measurements, reproduced

The licensed figure-data archive for Farr and colleagues' SBW25 hotspot study is now acquired and checksum verified. Original code reconstructs 24 culture occurrences and passes 986 source arithmetic, formula and mapping checks. The pooled corrected terminal-frequency ratio between reporter backgrounds is about 118.97. The authors' reported MSS mutation-rate point estimates instead imply 58.33. These are different quantities: mutant descendants and observation affect terminal frequencies. The mutation-rate fit and its intervals were not reproduced.

Keep the assay and sampling gaps visible

The transcript reconstruction gives a 5.9603 geometric-mean ratio of regional transcript proxies under the supplied ideal-doubling transform; it does not calibrate native mutation supply. One culture row lists seven counted candidates but eight sequenced colonies, and the first sequencing occasion has 94 available reads against 95 workbook entries. These are open sampling and provenance questions. Ten measurement-contract gaps remain; colonies, sequence reads and repeated transformants cannot be relabelled as independent Wsp/Aws/Mws trials.

What a supply-transfer assumption would change

An ordinary algebraic calculation shows how prespecified native/reporter supply-error bounds would widen the existing route-contrast intervals together with recovery uncertainty. The dataset supplies no finite native/reporter bound for the three routes. Without such a justified bound, the calculation supplies no biological test. It establishes neither a new theorem nor an additional causal dominance mechanism.

Certified comparisons of frozen forecasts

A new engine gives rigorously bounded score comparisons for prespecified forecasts of observed qualifying Wsp/Aws/Mws outcomes. One simultaneous confidence region supports every frozen comparator. Twenty synthetic tests and 30,887 independent exact review assertions passed; the stored scoring example is inconclusive. These checks verify the bounded numerical procedure; independent founders, justified count laws and predictions fixed before outcomes still need a real study contract.

A precision limit, before outcomes

A conservative illustration targeting a 0.05-nat interval half-width requires 527 and 921 qualifying outcomes in its two contexts, exceeding the prototype's 500-unit per-context cap. This is a bound on interval width, not a power result or a recommended biological sample size.

New source checks strengthen competing explanations

Full-text inspection of Farr and colleagues (2025), Barber and Couce (2026), and Li and Gyorgy (2025) examines transcription-dependent mutation supply, population history and plasmid masking. Farr's engineered SBW25 reporter shows about a 58-fold difference in reported fluctuation-rate estimates across the compared backgrounds; that does not calibrate native Wsp/Aws/Mws supply. The review separates mutation production from establishment and phenotypic visibility. The Farr figure-data archive is now audited separately above; the Barber/Couce and Li/Gyorgy datasets remain unacquired. No complete matched Wsp/Aws/Mws panel is admitted within the inspected scope.

Recovery uncertainty, including all-success controls

Methods extension 0.5.0 adds conservative confidence bounds for recovery-corrected Wsp/Aws/Mws route comparisons. All-success controls still leave uncertainty; observed zeros can produce unbounded ranges. The package includes 13 automated tests and 160 synthetic studies. No complete matched biological route/control panel has been authenticated within the inspected material.

Correcting the source record

The Wsp/Aws/Mws counts 46/41/18 in Lind et al. (2019) came from separate pathway-isolated strains. They cannot be treated as competing-route winner frequencies.

Context in published observations

A secondary analysis restores the three experimental blocks in the published rpoB table. Medium-specific predictions outperform a pooled prediction in each held-out block, with an exploratory total gain of 13.126 bits across 111 isolates. This does not identify the proposed TMD mechanism.

A test of changing route fractions

A context-stratified timing diagnostic accounts for right censoring. Selected synthetic null scenarios rejected 12/200 and 9/200 datasets at a 5% threshold; a deliberately strong switching alternative was detected in 200/200. These are simulations of a procedure, not biological experiments.

A limit on identifying mechanisms

The same Lomax first-event waiting-time distribution and constant route shares can arise from different rate constructions. First-event observations alone cannot distinguish those mechanisms.

What natural dominance means here

TMD describes a route as more naturally dominant in a specified context when it has a higher rate of producing a defined successful heritable outcome. Mutation supply, accessibility and establishment can all contribute. This differs from genetic dominance in a heterozygote. Observed winner frequencies alone cannot separate these causes, selection, drift or recovery bias. The empirical question is whether a prespecified cross-context restriction predicts held-out observations after independently measured supply and justified recovery correction. A fitted route preference alone does not establish a new causal mechanism.

Assumptions and open questions

The timing diagnostic requires independent units, independent censoring conditional on context, exact first successful arrival times and time bins chosen before inspecting outcomes. Endpoint counts in the rpoB table do not satisfy those timing requirements. Failure to reject constant route shares does not prove TMD. The newer confidence method also requires justified count laws, introduced control totals, prespecified sampling and evidence that relative control recovery transfers to biological samples. Overlapping intervals do not establish equivalence or identify a mechanism. Certified forecast scores additionally require a complete prespecified endpoint ledger and justified conditional count laws, with Other, no-qualified-outcome, unresolved and missing outcomes retained. Zero qualifying outcomes are unevaluable; correlated descendants or forecasts fitted after outcomes do not satisfy the current contract.
