Unified Theory of Everything

Could the Universe’s many rules come from something deeper? This research program makes selected calculations public: neutrino kernels, checked reductions of a quantum-loop seed, a conditional scalar continuum and a changing-mass toy model.

Research status

An unfinished research program with limited mathematical and computational results. A complete theory of everything and an experimentally confirmed new law are not established.

What the public work reports

Selected neutrino kernels

The package supplies an exact finite-pole tree expression, local moments, a remainder and known-node reconstruction. Masses and couplings remain inputs; unknown poles need additional information.

A corrected quantum-loop claim

One derivative seed has an evaluated off-shell ultraviolet polynomial. The earlier physical dimension-seven coefficient and all-odd extension remain withdrawn.

Local operators and interaction contacts

The October 1 checkpoint maps that polynomial to six local, electroweak-covariant dimension-seven monomials. Equations with interaction sources retain contacts that a free-field replacement misses. A correlated Yukawa and contact cancellation holds for arbitrary complex flavor coefficients under the declared change of variables; it does not establish physical running or cancellation of the full seed packet.

An explicit gauge-field-strength term leaves the gauge-free three-point restriction unchanged while affecting a four-point amplitude. The three-point result therefore cannot determine the full gauge completion, and the six monomials are a representative rather than a complete independent basis.

Conditional scalar results

The scalar continuum work gives a finite-moment criterion, nonanalytic low-energy behavior and a positive single-pole lower bound, under inherited spectral premises.

A changing-mass diagnostic

The toy model provides a positive weak-pulse occupation and solver comparisons. Its background is prescribed externally; it supplies neither a complete cosmic energy account nor a matter–antimatter asymmetry.

Assumptions and open questions

A complete independent operator basis, matching of additional gauge and multi-field amplitudes, reduction of the full sourced packet, physical running and finite thresholds remain unfinished. A complete source-to-observable susceptibility is also missing. Exact arithmetic and replay checks support the declared identities and toy models; they do not establish a new interaction, an observational prediction, experimental validation or independent human peer review.
