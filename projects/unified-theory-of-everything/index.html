Unified Theory of Everything

Could the Universe’s many rules come from something deeper? This research program makes selected calculations available: neutrino kernels, proposed reductions of a quantum-loop seed, a conditional scalar continuum and a changing-mass toy model.

Research status

An unfinished research program with limited mathematical and computational results. A complete theory of everything and an experimentally confirmed new law are not established.

The October 1 operator checkpoint is now included in the public repository after the research update was merged. The fixed source version supplies the October 1 operator results summarized below. A GitHub merge does not establish independent human peer review or experimental validation.

A provisional October 15–30 continuation is tracked in research PR #2. Its restricted gauge, operator and neutrino-Yukawa calculations remain under review and are described separately below.

What the source materials report

Selected neutrino kernels

The package supplies an exact finite-pole tree expression, local moments, a remainder and known-node reconstruction. Masses and couplings remain inputs; unknown poles need additional information.

A corrected quantum-loop claim

One derivative seed has an evaluated off-shell ultraviolet polynomial. The earlier physical dimension-seven coefficient and all-odd extension remain withdrawn.

Local operators and interaction contacts

The October 1 checkpoint maps that polynomial to six local, electroweak-covariant dimension-seven monomials. Equations with interaction sources retain contacts that a free-field replacement misses. A correlated Yukawa and contact cancellation holds for arbitrary complex flavor coefficients under the declared change of variables; it does not establish physical running or cancellation of the full seed packet.

An explicit gauge-field-strength term leaves the gauge-free three-point restriction unchanged while affecting a four-point amplitude. The three-point result therefore cannot determine the full gauge completion, and the six monomials are a representative rather than a complete independent basis.

Conditional scalar results

The scalar continuum work gives a finite-moment criterion, nonanalytic low-energy behavior and a positive single-pole lower bound, under inherited spectral premises.

A changing-mass diagnostic

The toy model provides a positive weak-pulse occupation and solver comparisons. Its background is prescribed externally; it supplies neither a complete cosmic energy account nor a matter–antimatter asymmetry.

Provisional calculation · under review

October 15–30 gauge, operator and matter continuation

The candidate calculates a nonzero transverse part of one restricted four-point loop residue: a gauge interaction that the earlier three-point result could not determine. It fixes the action and charge-conjugation sign conventions, then classifies five allowed transverse tensor directions under stated off-shell assumptions. These five directions are not a complete independent effective-field-theory operator basis.

The candidate now also evaluates all seven one-loop diagrams in the same restricted sector with two external hypercharge fields. Two independently implemented reductions give the same ultraviolet divergence and match the chosen local interaction terms. The calculation includes the contact coupling of both gauge fields to the scalar required by its kinetic action, and the matched divergence is independent of the scalar mass.

A further calculation includes the weak-isospin partner. Combining the two gauge contributions cancels the selected neutral-photon ultraviolet pole after inserting a constant neutral Higgs background. The corresponding Z and charged weak coefficients can remain nonzero. This result concerns the local divergence; the finite full photon amplitude remains uncalculated.

New calculations retain ordered weak generators in the two-W and mixed hypercharge–weak loops, then evaluate all 33 matter-loop diagrams with three external gauge fields. Two separately implemented loop integrations agree and match the covariant local action. Power counting makes primitive diagrams with four or more external gauge fields ultraviolet finite at the lowest gauge-coupling order. This covers every potentially divergent external-gauge multiplicity in the selected one-loop seed/Yukawa matter sector; other matter sectors and the physical coefficient map remain open.

A calculated local ultraviolet contact containing a photon and two charged weak fields can remain nonzero despite the selected one-photon pole cancellation. The contact supplies no finite photon amplitude or scattering prediction.

The continuation keeps the interaction sources generated by the local operators, including scalar and retained fermion currents. The source-retaining reduction now covers both weak isospin and hypercharge, with explicit local changes of the lepton, Higgs, sterile-neutrino and real gauge fields. A certificate tracks the equation terms removed by these first-order changes. The displayed curvature coefficient remains −1/12 in this chosen representative; it does not predict a physical dipole or transition moment.

Identical-fermion exchange involves both weak and flavor indices. A charged weak source can survive even with one active flavor; the flavor-only simplification of the hypercharge source cannot be applied to it. On a constant neutral Higgs background, the selected field changes shift the Z and charged weak coordinates while leaving the photon coordinate unchanged. This coordinate statement does not remove the previously calculated photon–W⁺–W⁻ contact or determine a photon amplitude.

An algebraic reduction now separates the selected terms containing two active leptons, two Higgs fields and one field strength into three hypercharge and nine weak coefficient coordinates for three active flavors. A separate reduction treats the Higgs and four-fermion current terms. For identical lepton fields, the spinor rearrangement identifies contractions while exchanging flavor labels. These are independent coordinates within the declared nonderivative field sectors, before relations involving derivatives or equations of motion.

The symmetric seed imposes further flavor constraints at a fixed neutrino Yukawa matrix. The raw symmetric weak source determines its antisymmetric counterpart, including when the Yukawa matrix has deficient rank. A checked sharp algebraic bound limits the antisymmetric source norm by the symmetric source norm. These maps transport only the explicit current and curvature-source contributions in the chosen representative; the remaining derivative, scalar and source terms can also contribute to the same final operators. The bound does not constrain measured moments or complete amplitudes.

A specified local differential change of variables extends the previous neutral kinetic normalization to interacting fields. Direct substitution separates the part removed by that normalization from the remaining scalar-derivative and curvature interactions, while retaining induced Majorana-mass and source terms. Derivatives act on varying Higgs fields and entire composite sources. In this convention and at this order, the previously matched two- and three-gauge local kernels retain their coefficients. The source reduction and normalization are two specified coordinate descriptions of the original packet. The October 16 continuation below constructs their first-order bridge while transforming the complete action and its sources. The calculation does not canonically normalize the entire interacting operator.

A conditional lower-order benchmark also shows how an optional dimension-six normalization and dimension-five mass operator induce a dimension-seven contact. Its dimension-five and dimension-six coefficients are declared inputs; the induced contact supplies a consistency check at order seven.

October 16 action and source completion

The continuation now gives an explicit first-order bridge between the source-reduced fields and the specified local normalization, including every declared field and its sources. Direct substitution checks the full neutral quadratic action off shell. When the Higgs background is not stationary, the nonlinear scalar shift contributes a scalar-potential term to the quadratic fermion action. Retaining that term makes the coordinate comparison agree. The two maps describe related actions; their induced coefficient tables cannot simply be added.

The scalar gauge-current contribution reduces to two retained derivative structures plus interaction-source terms. Those sources generate active scalar terms, retained-mass terms and additional scalar terms containing sterile neutrinos. A separate substitution transports charged-lepton and quark Yukawa interactions through the chosen maps, retaining the induced derivative contacts. These are classical action identities for selected contributions; other terms can feed the same final operators. Read the October 16 action and source guide for derivations, checks and limitations.

October 16 derivative placement and source projection

Separately implemented calculations resolve the selected terms containing a gauge field strength and a derivative on the Higgs. They identify a specific chiral combination of the strength and its dual in the declared convention. Moving the derivative to the lepton changes the displayed tensor coefficient from −1/12 to +1/6, together with correlated current and interaction-source terms. The complete sourced action remains equivalent; the changed coefficient does not imply a changed physical prediction.

A further calculation collects every term with no retained sterile field in the original sourced representative for the declared neutrino-Yukawa action and optional scalar quartic. Derivatives remain on whole Higgs–lepton composites, and additional Standard Model spectator sources have their own checked remainder. This field-content projection and an equation-of-motion reduction can give different coefficient blocks because the field changes mix active and sterile coordinates. Setting a retained field to zero does not integrate out a particle or determine physical coefficients.

The source recurrences also allow constant complex symmetric Majorana masses, with the adjoints and ordering of their mass products retained. These are selected classical action and coefficient-transport results. The derivative and source-projection guide records the independent checks, declared source theory and remaining physical-matching requirements.

October 16 neutrino-Yukawa loop inputs

Two separately implemented ultraviolet integrations now agree on the lepton, sterile-neutrino and Higgs two-point poles in the sector containing only neutrino Yukawa couplings. They retain the Higgs mass pole from the sterile mass, the weak multiplicities and both Majorana chiral channels. A separate graph and chirality analysis finds no ultraviolet pole in the proper one-loop Yukawa vertex in this sector. An allowed triangle containing an anomalous Majorana mass propagator is ultraviolet finite; its finite amplitude is not calculated.

Combining those field and vertex results reproduces the known one-loop running of the renormalizable neutrino Yukawa coupling, Majorana mass and Higgs mass in this restricted coupling sector. This is a benchmark against established results. Gauge couplings, other Yukawa couplings, the scalar quartic and higher-dimension operator insertions are outside this calculation.

Direct substitution also determines the external-field counterterm of the dimension-seven seed and converts it to a running input using its dimension in dimensional regularization. At that checkpoint, the proper seed vertices and operator mixing still required calculation; the field contribution alone supplied no complete dimension-seven beta function or physical prediction. The neutrino-Yukawa counterterm guide records the conventions, independent calculations, literature benchmarks and remaining requirements.

October 23 derivative-seed matter loops

New one-loop calculations insert the original dimension-seven derivative seed together with two neutrino Yukawa interactions. An exhaustive graph classification finds no proper vertex correction to the original lepton-number-violating two-lepton/two-Higgs term at this coupling order. This closes a specified coefficient projection in the unreduced action coordinates. Other connected diagrams join an earlier source loop to a Yukawa interaction through a sterile propagator. These contributions must be retained before physical coefficients can be collected.

Other allowed graphs use a Majorana mass insertion. Two independent integrations agree on a nonzero four-lepton contact pole. Two more independent integrations agree on four-Higgs poles: a derivative interaction linear in the retained mass and a scalar-potential term cubic in that mass. These are ultraviolet counterterms generated by the declared seed and couplings while the sterile field remains in the theory. They do not predict the input mass, a measured Higgs property or a finite scattering amplitude.

The Higgs derivative coefficient is complex, and its conjugate interaction is retained in the Hermitian action. A local scalar phase change shifts the term proportional to its imaginary part and induces Yukawa and external-probe source terms that must be retained. The primitive four-lepton contribution also has an exact flavor-image constraint, with rank at most two in each weak-triplet block; additional action contributions can change the total coefficient and its rank.

The remaining mixed two-lepton/two-Higgs Majorana-mass amplitude has a different conjugate-field arrangement and still needs integration. Its derivative placements have different ultraviolet degrees, so a graph classification alone does not determine its coefficient. The derivative-seed matter-loop guide provides the pole calculations, independent checks, source transport and literature comparisons. Complete physical running, finite matching and an observable remain open.

October 26 mixed lepton–Higgs loop

A new one-loop calculation evaluates the mixed lepton/Higgs matter channel with one derivative seed and two neutrino Yukawa interactions. Two independently checked integration routes agree on a mass-dependent ultraviolet pole when the internal fields attach to opposite parts of the seed's composite operator. When they attach to the same part, the derivative's loop momentum cancels and that placement is ultraviolet finite.

The computed local pole depends on a complex flavor product containing the retained Majorana mass. A separate checked action identity keeps the interaction sources, equation terms and scalar current when transporting this term between field coordinates. The calculation is made with zero external gauge fields; the physical coefficient still requires the remaining source and operator reduction, other contributions and a chosen subtraction scheme. A finite amplitude and an observable remain undetermined.

The mixed lepton–Higgs calculation guide records the pole, independent audit and source identity. These internal checks do not establish a complete physical running calculation, an experimentally confirmed law or unification.

Correlated field changes and interaction sources

A further calculation combines selected terms from the preceding lepton and scalar poles. Coordinated changes of the lepton and Higgs fields remove their specified imaginary-current terms in the declared first-order action. The resulting neutrino, charged-lepton and quark interactions and independent probe sources are carried through the change. A flavor analysis identifies when the neutrino interaction is unchanged by this particular field choice. With two or more independent coupled flavor directions, a nonzero change of this kind also changes that interaction. With one coupled direction, a special nonzero change can leave the neutrino interaction unchanged; charged-lepton and quark terms may still change.

The combined matter and source guide gives the algebra and its checks. This is a selected classical action-coordinate result based on the previously calculated poles. A complete quantum source map, physical running and finite observables remain open.

October 30 normalization and remaining scalar interactions

The next calculation extends the selected field changes to the real lepton and Higgs normalization terms. It carries the resulting neutrino, charged-lepton and quark interactions, scalar potential and independent probe sources through the same first-order action. If a separate dimension-five coefficient is supplied as an input, its induced dimension-seven term is retained as well. This calculation uses the earlier loop poles and performs no new loop integration.

The remaining scalar derivative terms define a curved metric on the space of scalar field values when their real coefficient is nonzero. An explicit tensor calculation gives the same curvature before and after the radial field change. This establishes a limited obstruction: a local scalar field reparametrization containing no derivatives cannot remove all of these selected derivative interactions. The curvature belongs to scalar field space; it supplies no spacetime-gravity result or complete physical prediction.

The real matter normalization and geometry guide records the action map, independent checks and scope. The remaining current and contact terms, earlier source counterterms and other field and coupling inputs still need collection in a complete operator basis before physical running or observables can be extracted.

Exact arithmetic and separately implemented internal checks support the declared identities. The field redefinitions and specified local normalization cover selected first-order changes with interaction remainders retained. A complete retained-sterile operator basis for the surviving matter and current terms, full interacting canonical normalization, other diagram sectors, remaining counterterms, quantum source transport, subtraction choices and finite matching are still needed before extracting physical running or observables. No new fundamental mathematics, established unification or external novelty is claimed. Read the gauge calculation, the action and operator continuation, the two-field loop calculation, the electroweak and source continuation, the explicit field changes and neutral normalization, the non-Abelian matching and photon-contact calculation, the interacting electroweak reduction and local normalization, the algebraic operator sectors and seed flavor constraints and research PR #2 for scope and review status.

Assumptions and open questions

A complete independent retained-sterile operator basis for the surviving matter and current terms, other external-matter loop sectors, full interacting canonical normalization, physical running and finite thresholds remain unfinished. A complete source-to-observable susceptibility is also missing. Exact arithmetic and replay checks support the declared identities and toy models; they do not establish a new interaction, an observational prediction, experimental validation or independent human peer review.
