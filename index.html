How to read the work

Claims you can trace.
Limits you can see.

These programs ask different questions and retain separate assumptions. Their presence in one collection does not establish a single combined theory.

01

Start with the status

Each project distinguishes its research hypothesis, reported calculations and unresolved scientific questions.

02

Follow the primary materials

Repository documentation, source code and publication archives provide the details behind these summaries. Read the assumptions alongside the results.

03

Use the right version

Archive identifiers refer to specific publications or version series. Older baselines and later research editions are distinguished on each project page.

04

Separate checks from validation

AI-assisted preparation and computational audits are disclosed in project documentation. They do not constitute independent human peer review.
